A piece composed and tracked in my home studio setup, applying principles from my minor in Music Recording Techniques at Universidad de los Andes.

### Production Notes
- **Tracking:** Direct-in electric guitars processed through custom impulse responses and valve-modeled preamps.
- **Mixdown:** Strict gain staging, mid-side EQ separation for stereo width, and multiband compression on the master bus targeting -14 LUFS integrated loudness.
