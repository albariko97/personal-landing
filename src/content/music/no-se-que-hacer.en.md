"No Sé Qué Hacer" is Álvaro's official debut single, written, performed, tracked, and produced end-to-end. Applying techniques from his minor in Music Recording Techniques at Universidad de los Andes, the track balances raw acoustic warmth with modern punch.

### Production Notes
- **Guitars & Tracking:** Layered acoustic rhythms combined with atmospheric electric guitars processed through custom impulse responses.
- **Mix & Mastering:** Precise gain staging, subtractive EQ carving, and multi-band dynamic control calibrated for multi-platform digital streaming via Amuse.
