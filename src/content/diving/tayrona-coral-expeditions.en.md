Where the Sierra Nevada de Santa Marta meets the ocean, underwater landscapes transform into dramatic boulder gardens and current-swept rocky passages.

### Diver Reflections
- **Current Management:** Adapting streamlining and kick cadence to ocean currents reinforces the value of adaptive systems that gracefully absorb turbulence.
- **Ecological Conservation:** Seeing coral restoration efforts up close reinforces a deep appreciation for fragile, interdependent ecosystems.
